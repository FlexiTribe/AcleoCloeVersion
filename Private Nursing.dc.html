Questions

Private nursing FAQs

How much does a private nurse cost?+

Weekday visits start from $120 for 30 minutes. Your first visit is an Initial Nursing Visit from $165 (minimum 1 hour). All rates are on our pricing page.

Do I need a referral to book a nurse?+

Not at all. Anyone can book Acleo directly — you, a family member or a carer — by calling 1300 225 366 or enquiring online. If you're coming home from hospital or have a specific treatment plan, we'll connect with your GP, specialist or ward team so your nursing care follows their instructions.

How soon can a nurse visit?+

Same-day appointments are available where possible. If you're coming home from hospital, call us and we'll prioritise it.

Who will visit me?+

Registered and Enrolled Nurses, each working within their scope of practice, with Registered Nurse oversight. Your Acleo Nurse Partner oversees your care.

What if my usual nurse is away?+

Another Acleo nurse who works from the same notes and care plan will visit, so you don't have to start again.

What's the difference between a nurse and a support worker?+

Nurses are AHPRA-registered and provide clinical care such as dressings, injections and catheter care. Support workers help with non-clinical tasks — at Acleo, they assist with appointments and transport.

Can I use NDIS or Support at Home funding?+

Yes — for self- and plan-managed NDIS participants, and by working alongside your Support at Home provider.
