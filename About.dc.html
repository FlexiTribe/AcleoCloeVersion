Acleo – How We Support You

Exceptional care starts with clarity, honesty, and a Registered Nurse who genuinely understands your situation — not unrealistic promises, rushed handovers, or hidden costs.

Your Nurse Partner takes time to learn your health needs, routines, preferences, and what matters most. This creates care that feels coordinated, personal, and clinically guided — without pretending we can do everything.

✓A thorough first assessment of your health, home and daily rhythm

✓A care plan written with you, in plain language
Keeping a clinical eye on things
✓Scheduled reviews so your plan keeps pace with you

Keeping everyone connected

The person behind Acleo

Chloe

Registered Nurse · Founder of Acleo

I started Acleo because care should feel personal. Too often people are moved through a system rather than looked after within it. With Acleo, you speak directly to a nurse and care begins when you need it. And because we're independent — not a franchise — your support is shaped around you.

I'd seen large providers charging for time people didn't need, without proper clinical care. I wanted something clinically led and local — fair, focused on getting the most out of your care, and helping you stay in your own home.

"Our mission is simple: to help you stay at home."

Four things I was determined to get right
Charge fairly for the time your care takesVisits are sized to the care actually needed, so more of your hours stay available for the support you want.
Nursing care overseen by a Registered NurseWhoever comes to you, a Registered Nurse holds oversight of the clinical care.
Start care straight awayAcleo can often begin nursing care while package or provider arrangements are still being sorted out.
Support families, not just patientsMaking the load at home manageable, so your loved one can stay home where it's safe to do so.
Who delivers your care

Nursing care is provided by Registered and Enrolled Nurses, with any Enrolled Nurse care overseen by a Registered Nurse, and Support Workers assisting with appointments and transport. Whoever comes to you works from the same notes and the same standard — so you're not starting again each visit.
