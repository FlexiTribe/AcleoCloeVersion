HOSPITAL DISCHARGE

Planning Ahead for Your Hospital Discharge

If you know you're going to be discharged from hospital, Acleo can help you prepare before you return home.

If you know you're being discharged from hospital, contact Acleo before you go home. With your consent, one of our nurses can liaise with your treating hospital team to support a safe, well-planned transition — understanding your clinical needs, reviewing your discharge plan and ensuring appropriate nursing care can commence as soon as possible.

Our goal is to ensure your nursing care is organised before you leave hospital, so you can return home with confidence knowing the right support is already in place. By planning ahead, we can help ensure there is a smooth transition from hospital to home, with the right nursing care arranged from day one.

We understand that sometimes people leave hospital before community services, nursing visits or essential supplies have been arranged. Our goal is to help make your transition home as safe, well-planned and stress-free as possible.

Unfortunately, we occasionally meet people who have been discharged home without nursing services commencing or with limited wound care supplies to support their recovery. At Acleo, we aim to minimise these gaps by working with your treating team before discharge whenever possible, helping to ensure appropriate nursing care and clinical planning are in place before you arrive home.
