Book a visit See pricing

How we support older clients

Regular health checks

Blood pressure, weight, skin and mobility reviewed on a set schedule.

Medication oversight

Webster-pak checks, side-effect monitoring and pharmacist liaison.

Falls prevention

Home safety review, strength checks and equipment recommendations.

Memory & dementia care

Calm, familiar nurses and routines that reduce distress.

Skin & wound care

Early treatment of skin tears and pressure areas before they worsen.

Palliative support

Comfort-focused care at home, alongside your GP and palliative team.
